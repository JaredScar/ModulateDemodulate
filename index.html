                    How NRZI works
                    
The message is turned into UTF-8 bytes, then into 8-bit groups. ASCII text is one byte per character. The line idles at 0.20. A 1 flips the line between 0.20 and 0.50. A 0 leaves it where it was. Demodulation reads that backwards: a change is a 1, a hold is a 0.
